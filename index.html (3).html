Adverbial Linkers

أدوات الربط الظرفية – تربط الجمل وتوضّح العلاقة بينها (سبب / نتيجة / تناقض / وقت)
What are they?
Words/phrases that connect sentences to show a logical relationship.

Contrast: However, Although, Even though, Despite, Nevertheless
Result: Therefore, As a result, Consequently
Addition: Furthermore, Moreover, In addition
Time: Meanwhile, Eventually, Afterwards
Condition: Unless (= if not)
Examples
Contrast

I was tired. However, I finished my work.

كنت متعبًا، ومع ذلك أنهيت عملي.

Result

He studied hard. As a result, he passed the exam.

ذاكر بجد. ونتيجةً لذلك، نجح.

Condition

Unless you hurry, you will miss the bus.

إذا لم تسرع، ستفوتك الحافلة.

Quick Tip 💡
However / Therefore → capital + comma after.   Although / Unless → followed by subject + verb clause.

Third Conditional

الشرط الثالث – مواقف خيالية في الماضي – شيء لم يحدث وكيف كانت النتائج ستختلف
Structure
If + Past Perfect  ,  would have + Past Participle

• if-clause: had + V3
• result: would / could / might + have + V3
Both refer to an unreal past situation.
Examples
Example 1

If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam.

لو كنت ذاكرت بجد أكثر، لكنت نجحت.

Example 2

If she had left earlier, she would not have missed the train.

لو كانت خرجت أبكر، ما كانت فاتها القطار.

Common Mistake ⚠️
Never use would have in the if-clause.
✗ "If I would have studied…"    ✓ "If I had studied…"

Past Perfect Simple Passive

المبني للمجهول في الماضي التام – الفاعل مجهول والحدث وقع قبل حدث ماضٍ آخر
Structure
had + been + Past Participle (V3)

• Subject receives the action (does not do it).
• Action happened before another past event.
• Add by + agent if the doer is mentioned.
Examples
Example 1

The project had been completed before the meeting.

كان المشروع قد تم إنجازه قبل الاجتماع.

Example 2

The birds had been protected by scientists before release.

كانت الطيور قد تم حمايتها قبل الإفراج عنها.

Active vs Passive
Active: Scientists had protected the birds.
Passive: The birds had been protected.

Used to / Would

used to / would – عادات وأفعال متكررة في الماضي لم تعد تحدث الآن
Used to
Used to + base verb

• Past habits OR past states no longer true.
• Works with action AND state verbs.
Negative: didn't use to  |  Question: Did you use to…?
Example

We used to play football every Friday.

كنا نلعب كرة القدم كل يوم جمعة.

Would
Would + base verb

• Repeated past actions only (NOT states).
✗ "I would know him."   ✓ "I used to know him."
Common with: every summer, on Fridays, whenever…
Example

Every summer, we would travel to Alexandria.

كل صيف كنا نسافر إلى الإسكندرية.

Key Difference ⚡
Used to = habits + states  |  Would = repeated actions only

Phrasal Verbs

الأفعال المركبة – فعل + حرف جر / ظرف، ومعناها مختلف عن الفعل الأصلي
Key Phrasal Verbs
look for = search for (يبحث عن)
look into = investigate (يحقق في)
look at = direct eyes toward (ينظر إلى)
come across = find by chance (يصادف بالصدفة)
look after = take care of (يهتم بـ)
find out = discover (يكتشف)
pick up = collect / learn quickly
Examples
look for

I looked for my keys everywhere.

بحثت عن مفاتيحي في كل مكان.

come across

She came across a small injured fox behind the rocks.

صادفت ثعلبًا صغيرًا مصابًا خلف الصخور.

Word Order 💡
Separable: "look it up" ✓   Inseparable: never "come it across" ✗

Reported Speech

الكلام المنقول – ننقل كلام شخص ما مع تغيير الضمائر والأزمنة والكلمات الظرفية
Tense Backshift
Present Simple → Past Simple
Present Continuous → Past Continuous
Past Simple → Past Perfect
Will → Would  |  Can → Could  |  May → Might
Statement
Direct → Reported

"I am tired." → Sara said that she was tired.

قالت سارة إنها كانت متعبة.

Reported Questions
Yes/No → use whether / if + normal word order (no ?)
Wh- → keep the question word + normal word order
Example

Omar asked the guide whether he had seen the fox.

سأل عمر المرشد عمّا إذا كان قد رأى الثعلب.

Time / Place Changes
now→then  |  today→that day  |  tomorrow→the next day  |  here→there  |  this→that

Past Perfect Continuous

الماضي التام المستمر – فعل كان مستمرًا لفترة قبل حدث آخر في الماضي
Structure
had + been + verb-ing

• Action in progress for a duration before another past event.
• Key words: for, since, all day, for hours, the whole time…
Examples
Example 1

They had been studying for hours before the exam started.

كانوا يذاكرون لساعات قبل أن يبدأ الامتحان.

Example 2

The scientists had been observing the animals for several hours.

كان العلماء يراقبون الحيوانات لعدة ساعات.

Vs. Past Perfect Simple
Past Perfect Simple = focus on completion
Past Perfect Continuous = focus on duration / process
